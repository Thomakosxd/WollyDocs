# 🍵 Basic Setup

Wolly is designed to be straightforward and quick to set up[cite: 1]. If you just want the essentials, you can get everything running without spending time on complex configurations[cite: 1].

---

## Out of the box experience <Icon name="rocket" />

The moment you invite Wolly to your server, the Bumper module is ready to track Disboard activity automatically[cite: 1]!

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick Start">
  No complex setup required to get started! Wolly handles detection out of the box[cite: 1].
</Callout>

### How it works

<Steps>

1. **Member Initiates Bump**
   A member types `/bump` using the Disboard bot[cite: 1].

2. **Wolly Detects Activity**
   Wolly detects the successful bump confirmation in chat[cite: 1].

3. **Timer Started**
   Wolly starts a 2-hour timer in the background and posts a confirmation message mentioning when the next bump is available[cite: 1].

4. **Primary Reminder Sent**
   When the 2 hours are up, Wolly sends a reminder embed directly to your configured reminder channel, mentioning your designated ping role (or the last bumper)[cite: 1].

5. **Second Reminder Triggered**
   If 30 minutes pass after the server becomes ready and no one bumps, Wolly triggers a **Second Reminder** in the channel to keep your growth on track[cite: 1].

</Steps>
