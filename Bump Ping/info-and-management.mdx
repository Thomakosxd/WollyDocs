# 🛠️ Managing Your Setup

Once you have set up your channels, roles, and status, you can easily double-check your current configurations or make changes down the road.

---

## Check Your Settings

Not sure what channel is set for reminders or what settings are active? Simply run:

```text
/bumpping info
```

This command displays a clean panel showing:

- **Reminder Channel:** The text channel assigned for bump alerts.
- **Ping Role:** The role currently set to be mentioned.
- **Status Channel & Prefix:** The voice status channel and its display text.
- **Second Reminder:** Status (`ON`/`OFF`) and the custom delay timer.
- **Embed Timestamps:** Status (`ON`/`OFF`) for exact time indicators.
- **Embed Details:** Your saved custom embed title and description.

---

## Starting Fresh

If you ever want to completely reset the Bumper module for your server, the **Server Owner** can run:

```text
/bumpping deletealldata
```

> **Warning:** This command permanently wipes all stored bump configurations (channels, roles, status settings, and custom embeds) for your server. To prevent accidental data loss, Wolly requires a 3-step confirmation before deleting anything.
