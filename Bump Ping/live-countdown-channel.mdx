# ⏱️ Live Countdown Channel <Badge text="💎 Premium" variant="caution" />

Want your members to see when the next bump is ready at a glance[cite: 4]? Wolly can create a locked voice channel that displays the remaining cooldown timer directly in your channel list[cite: 4].

---

## How to Enable It

Simply run the command[cite: 4]:

```text
/bumpping statuschannel

```

Wolly will automatically create a locked voice channel configured with your current status prefix.

---

## Customizing the Prefix

You can easily change the prefix or emojis displayed before the status text.

Run the command:

```text
/bumpping setstatusname name:<prefix>

```
