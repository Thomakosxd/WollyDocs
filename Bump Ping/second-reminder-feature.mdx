Sometimes a bump reminder gets buried in active chat channels. Wolly includes a built-in secondary ping system that sends a follow-up alert if your server hasn't been bumped after becoming ready.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Initial Cooldown Ends">
    Wolly sends the primary reminder after 2 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Timer Starts">
    A secondary timer begins counting down.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow-Up Trigger">
    If no one bumps the server within the set delay, Wolly sends a second alert to your reminder channel pinging your configured role (or the last bumper).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Auto-Reset">
    Once a successful `/bump` is detected, the second reminder state resets automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Enabling & Disabling

The second reminder is **ON by default**. You can toggle it at any time:

```text
/bumpping secondreminder status:ON
/bumpping secondreminder status:OFF
```

## Customizing the Delay

By default, Wolly waits **30 minutes** after the server becomes ready before triggering the second ping (2 hours \+ 30 minutes total).

You can customize this delay anywhere between **1 and 1440 minutes (24 hours)**:

```text
/bumpping setsecondreminderdelay minutes:<number>
```
