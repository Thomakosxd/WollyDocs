# Basic Setup

Wolly is designed to be straightforward and quick to set up. If you just want the essentials, you can get everything running without spending time on complex configurations.

---

## Out of the box experience

The moment you invite Wolly to your server, the Bumper module is ready to track Disboard activity automatically!

Here is how the default behavior works:

1. **A member types `/bump`** using the Disboard bot.
2. **Wolly detects the successful bump** confirmation in chat.
3. **Wolly starts a 2-hour timer** in the background and posts a confirmation message mentioning when the next bump is available.
4. **When the 2 hours are up**, Wolly sends a reminder embed directly to your configured reminder channel, mentioning your designated ping role (or the last bumper)\[cite: 1\].
5. **If 30 minutes pass** after the server becomes ready and no one bumps, Wolly triggers a **Second Reminder** in the channel to keep your growth on track\[cite: 1\].

---

## Recommended Quick Commands

If you want to customize where alerts go, you only need to run two quick commands:

- `/bumpping createchannel` – Creates a dedicated `#bump-here` text channel automatically\[cite: 1\].
- `/bumpping createrole` – Creates and assigns a mentionable `👊Bump ME` role for reminders\[cite: 1\].
