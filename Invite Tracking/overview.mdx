## Introduction

The **Invite Tracking** system allows you to automatically track who invited new members to your server. It calculates active joins, leaves, and bonus invites to provide real-time server growth statistics.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="User Statistics" icon="chart-simple">
    Check individual invite stats including joins, leaves, and bonus points.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Server Leaderboards" icon="trophy">
    Display interactive top-inviter leaderboards for your community.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Bonus Invite Control" icon="gift">
    Administrators can add, remove, or set custom bonus invites for members.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Real-Time Logs" icon="gem">
    Premium servers can send automated join logs to a designated channel.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Quick Start Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your stats">
    Use `/invitetracking user` in any channel to view your current invites.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View the Leaderboard">
    Type `/invitetracking leaderboard` to see who has brought the most members to the server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Logs 💎">
    If your server has **Premium**, use `/invitetracking setchannel` to enable join log notifications.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The system calculates **Net Invites** using the formula:<br />`Net Invites = (Joins + Bonus) - Leaves`
</Note>
