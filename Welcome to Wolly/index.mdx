## What can Wolly do?

Wolly includes a wide range of powerful modules to keep your community active, entertained, and secure:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Disboard Bumper" defaultOpen icon="hand-back-fist" iconType="regular">
    A fully automated system that detects when someone bumps your server, tracks the 2-hour cooldown, and sends reminders.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="📨Invite Tracker">
    A invite tracker system to track which members invite who and maybe reward them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Ready to start?

Getting started is as simple as inviting Wolly to your server. Check out the guides on the left sidebar to see how easy it is to use our commands!

<Tip>
  Wolly respects your server's hierarchy. Moderation commands automatically check permissions and roles, ensuring staff members can never take action against someone with a higher or equal role.
</Tip>
