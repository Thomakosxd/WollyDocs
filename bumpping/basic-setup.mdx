# 🚀 Plug & Play Bumping

Wolly is designed to be incredibly easy to use. You don't need to spend hours configuring settings if you just want the basics.

## Out of the box experience

If you simply invite Wolly to your server, the Bumper is already working! 

Here is how the default behavior works:
1. A member types `/bump` using the Disboard bot.
2. Wolly detects the successful bump.
3. Wolly starts a 2-hour timer in the background.
4. When the 2 hours are up, **Wolly will send a Direct Message (DM)** to the user who bumped, reminding them that it's time to bump again!

> **Note:** If you are happy with members just getting a private DM reminder, you don't need to do anything else! You are all set.
