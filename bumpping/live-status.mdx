# ⏱️ Live Countdown Channel

Want your members to see exactly when the next bump is ready, just by looking at your channel list? Wolly can create a locked Voice Channel that updates in real-time!

## How to enable it

Simply run the command:
**`/bumpping statuschannel`**

Wolly will automatically create a Voice Channel at the top of your server that looks like this:
`Bump: 1h 45m 20s`

When the timer hits zero, the channel will change to:
`Bump: READY!`

## Customizing the Prefix

Don't like the word "Bump:"? You can easily change the prefix of the channel name.

Use the command:
**`/bumpping setstatusname <name>`**

*Example:* If you type `/bumpping setstatusname Next Disboard:`, your channel will update to show: `Next Disboard: 1h 45m 20s`.
