# Meet Wolly 🤖

Welcome to the official documentation for **Wolly**! We built Wolly with one goal in mind: to make managing your Discord server as effortless as possible.

No complicated dashboards, no confusing setup processes. Just invite the bot, and it works instantly out of the box.

## What can Wolly do?

Wolly focuses on doing two things incredibly well:

* **👊 Disboard Bumper:** A fully automated system that detects when someone bumps your server, tracks the 2-hour cooldown, and sends reminders (via DM or public channels). It even creates live countdown voice channels!
* **🛡️ Moderation System:** A clean, built-in moderation suite that tracks staff actions. It includes user reporting, warnings, timeouts, kicks, and bans, all neatly organized into a case history database.

## Ready to start?

Getting started is as simple as inviting Wolly to your server. Check out the guides on the left sidebar to see how easy it is to use our commands!

> **Tip:** Wolly respects your server's hierarchy. Moderation commands automatically check permissions and roles, ensuring staff members can never take action against someone with a higher or equal role.
